# ACME Refund Policy

A short, fictional policy bundled as a sample document. Its central fact is
**conditional** — which is exactly what the Verify tab is built to catch.

## Eligibility

A widget is refundable **only if it is defective** and returned within 30 days
of delivery. Change-of-mind returns are not eligible for a refund.

## Processing

Refunds are processed within 5 business days of the returned item being
received. The refund is issued to the original payment method.

## Charges

Shipping charges are non-refundable. If a defective item is returned, ACME
covers the return shipping cost; otherwise the customer pays return shipping.
