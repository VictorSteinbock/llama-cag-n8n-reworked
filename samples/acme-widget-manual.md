# ACME Widget Pro — Owner's Manual

A short, fictional manual bundled as a sample document. Ingest it, then ask the
oracle questions and verify claims against it.

## Battery and power

The battery lasts 18 hours on a full charge. A full charge takes 90 minutes from
empty. The Widget charges over USB-C only; wireless charging is not supported.

## Operating conditions

Operating temperature range: 0 °C to 40 °C. Do not operate the Widget above
40 °C. Thermal shutdown triggers at 55 °C to protect the cell.

## Warranty

The warranty period is 24 months from the date of purchase. The warranty covers
manufacturing defects only; it does not cover water damage or accidental drops.

## Firmware

Firmware updates are delivered over Wi-Fi only; USB updates are not supported.
The Widget checks for updates automatically once per week.
